✈️ IAD → Budapest, PRG → IAD strategy
Neither leg has a nonstop from D.C. or to Prague, so this books as a Star Alliance connection through a European hub — most reliably Frankfurt or Munich on Lufthansa, or Vienna on Austrian (Vienna and Warsaw tend to have better business-class Saver availability than Frankfurt/Zurich, worth checking first). Since you're flying into Budapest and eventually home from Prague, this is an open-jaw — book it as one multi-city Star Alliance award in United's search tool rather than two separate one-ways.

Partner business-class awards to Europe have historically priced around 80,000 miles one-way, with United's own Polaris Saver sometimes as low as 60,000–77,000 when available — but United's pricing is dynamic and does shift, so treat this as a ballpark, not a quote. Search opens roughly 330 days out, which for an Aug 2027 trip means Saver space starts appearing around September 2026 — that's when to start watching.

If the United Club balance falls short: Chase Ultimate Rewards (Sapphire Reserve) transfers to MileagePlus at 1:1, instantly — the fastest way to top up a shortfall without waiting to earn more United miles directly.
